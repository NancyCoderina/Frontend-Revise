About Our Institute

Rajkiya Engineering College is a premier technical institute providing quality education in engineering and technology. The institute focuses on academic excellence, innovation and overall development of students.

Our Departments

Computer Science and Engineering

The CSE department provides education in programming, data structures, databases, artificial intelligence and web development.

Electronics Engineering

The Electronics Engineering department focuses on electronics, communication systems, microprocessors and digital electronics.

Mechanical Engineering

The Mechanical Engineering department deals with manufacturing, machines, thermodynamics and mechanical design.

Civil Engineering

The Civil Engineering department provides knowledge of construction, structural engineering, surveying and transportation engineering.
